# Property and land-plot (kavling) buyer UX and trust, with attention to Indonesia

## What information do property/land buyers need before contacting sales, and what builds or destroys trust?

### Takeaway
Buyers need price and total cost, location and risk, the plot's legal status (and whether it can be checked independently), developer credibility and real progress before they commit. Hidden pricing, forced data capture, stale or incomplete content, and unverifiable legal claims damage trust. In Indonesia the legal and delivery risk is concrete: split certificates, handover of documents, unbuilt projects.

Evidence tags used in all sections: **[F]** = I fetched and read the page (via a summarizing fetch tool). **[S]** = seen only in a search-result summary; re-open the page before quoting. **[WEAK]** = blog, vendor, or no primary source.

### Cited Findings

**General trust factors**
- [F] NN/g (Harley, 2016; builds on Nielsen's 1999 list, rechecked in a Singapore usability study) names four credibility factors: design quality; up-front disclosure; comprehensive, correct and current content; and connection to the rest of the web. — [NN/g, Trustworthiness in Web Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/)
- [F] Under up-front disclosure: show contact details, base costs, fees and return/guarantee policies before asking for personal information. Users often abandon sites that hide pricing, and distrusted a site that demanded an address before showing content. — [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/)
- [F] Typos and broken links read as carelessness. Users check outside sources (reviews, social media, news), view company-selected testimonials skeptically, and may doubt a business with no presence beyond its own site. Showing the service process and the people doing it signals expertise. — [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/trustworthy-design/)
- [S] NN/g advises saying clearly what is and is not included in each tier, because it reads as transparency. — [NN/g, B2B trust](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/b2b-trust-from-b2c/)

**What buyers weigh (Indonesia)**
- [S][WEAK] A property-media analysis says buyers filter by price and location first, then use developer reputation and project legality; searches also cover KPR (mortgage), facilities, transport access, flood risk and developer. No sample or method shown. — [Property Lounge](https://www.propertylounge.id/apa-yang-dicari-orang-sebelum-membeli-rumah-analisis-search-journey/)
- [S] Rumah123 data, as reported by media, describes 2025 consumers as active but more rational and selective; the coverage warns that a cheap price is not worth it if legality or access is problematic. These are about houses, not specifically plots. — [Iconomics](https://www.theiconomics.com/art-of-execution/laporan-rumah123-2025-jadi-tahun-penyeimbang-pasar-properti-siap-masuk-fase-baru-di-2026/), [Properti Terkini](https://propertiterkini.com/generasi-muda-serbu-properti-data-rumah123/)

- [S] A 2022 MeasuringU benchmark of US real estate sites listed inaccurate or untrustworthy information and design problems as common issues. — [MeasuringU](https://measuringu.com/real-estate-benchmark-2022/)

**What destroys trust in Indonesia**
- [F] YLKI received 1,977 complaints in 2025 (1,011 individual, 966 group); 919 of the group complaints came from housing consumers. Reported cases include homes never built after payment. YLKI's Tulus Abadi names full payment before construction as a key reason such cases recur and advises against it. Note: 919 is a count within group complaints and must not be read as 919 separate projects. — [Kompas, 20 Jul 2026](https://properti.kompas.com/read/2026/07/20/190000921/kenapa-konsumen-masih-saja-terjerat-rayuan-pengembang-nakal-)
- [S] A real case: a Kendari buyer found through BPN that the plot had a duplicate certificate; the police report (29 April 2025) was unresolved at time of publication. — [Kendariinfo](https://kendariinfo.com/laporan-dugaan-penipuan-jual-beli-tanah-kavling-di-kendari-belum-temui-titik-terang/)
- [S][WEAK] Guides list red flags for kavling fraud: seller refuses notary/BPN verification, forged or altered photocopied documents, land not in land-office records. — [99.co](https://www.99.co/id/panduan/ciri-ciri-penipuan-tanah-kavling/), [Adira Finance](https://adirafinance.co.id/admin/2025/02/07/ciri-ciri-penipuan-tanah-kavling/)
- [S][WEAK] Notary/PPAT guidance: a parent (induk) certificate that has not been split is not per-plot proof of ownership, and "will be split after you pay" is a known risk pattern. Check that the seller's name matches the certificate and verify at the land office or via PPAT before signing. The exact URL-to-claim mapping within this search was not confirmed. — [Notaris dan PPAT](https://notarisdanppat.com/case-study/beli-tanah-kavling/), [Lamudi](https://www.lamudi.co.id/journal/peraturan-tanah-kavling/)

**Legal terms and rules buyers meet**
- [S] SHM is full ownership with no time limit; SHGB is a right to build on state or another party's land for a limited term. For SHGB on state or HPL land: up to 30 years, extendable up to 20, renewable up to 30 (PP 18/2021). — [ANTARA, SHM vs SHGB](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4786221/pahami-perbedaan-shm-dan-shgb-sebelum-membeli-tanah-atau-rumah)
- [S] SHGB can be converted to SHM, with requirements and procedure shown in the Sentuh Tanahku app. Eligibility conditions apply and were not read. — [ATR/BPN](https://www.atrbpn.go.id/berita/masyarakat-dapat-mengubah-shgb-menjadi-shm-cek-persyaratan-dan-prosedurnya-di-aplikasi-sentuh-tanahku)
- [S] A company developer holds land as SHGB because only Indonesian citizens can generally hold hak milik (UUPA Art. 21). The search summary credited Hukumonline without identifying the article; re-verify. — [Hukumonline (probable article)](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/syarat-mengubah-hgb-rumah-tinggal-menjadi-hak-milik-cl1322/)
- [S] PPJB is a preliminary agreement used before the AJB, for example when the property is not ready or not fully paid. AJB is the PPAT deed recording the sale and the basis for transferring the certificate name; it is not itself proof of ownership like a certificate. — [Hukumonline PPJB](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/PPJB-dalam-proses-jual-beli-tanah-lt6086d9ebd6b6d/), [ATR/BPN Padangsidimpuan](https://kot-padangsidimpuan.atrbpn.go.id/pengumuman/apa-bedanya-ajb-dengan-ppbj)
- [S] Under PP 12/2021 a housing PPJB requires certainty on land status, the agreed items, PBG (building approval), infrastructure and utilities, and at least 20% construction progress; the land certificate is shown to the buyer at PPJB signing. The summaries cover houses and apartments only, not bare plots, and article numbering differed between sources. — [pasal.id Art. 22](https://pasal.id/peraturan/pp/pp-no-12-tahun-2021/pasal-22), [explainer](https://asriman.com/begini-sistem-ppjb-dalam-pemasaran-perumahan-menurut-pp-no-12-tahun-2021-sebagai-turunan-dari-uu-cipta-kerja/)
- [S] **Material legal flag:** UU 1/2011 Art. 146(1) bars a legal entity that builds a "lisiba" (ready-to-build environment) from selling mature plots without houses, with an exception for small plots in low-income (MBR) housing. pasal.id labels the article "amended" without saying how; I could not confirm its status after UU 6/2023. Its reach to other kinds of plot sellers is debated. — [pasal.id Art. 146](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-1-tahun-2011/pasal-146), [BPK copy of original law](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/28534/UU%201%20Tahun%202011.pdf)

**Independent verification channels (useful as trust features)**
- [F] ATR/BPN (24 June 2026): a certificate holder can share access in Sentuh Tanahku ("Sertipikatku" menu, "Bagikan Akses Sertipikat Ini"), entering the buyer's username or email and a viewing duration. The buyer sees it under "Dibagikan": last holder name, land area, location and registration history, including sales, grants, inheritance, mortgage or dispute blocks. — [ATR/BPN](https://www.atrbpn.go.id/berita/cek-informasi-tanah-sebelum-membeli-manfaatkan-fitur-berbagi-akses-di-aplikasi-sentuh-tanahku)
- [S] The app's "Cari Bidang" lookup (NIBEL for electronic certificates; right type, land office, village and number for analog ones) does not show owner name or area. If a parcel is not found it may not be digitally mapped yet, and the public is told to visit the land office. — [ATR/BPN](https://www.atrbpn.go.id/berita/jadi-lebih-mudah-cek-bidang-tanah-bisa-lewat-sentuh-tanahku)
- [S] QR codes on electronic certificates are to be scanned with the Sentuh Tanahku app. — [ATR/BPN Bantul](https://kab-bantul.atrbpn.go.id/berita/begini-cara-cek-keaslian-sertifikat-tanah-elektronik-simak-yuk)

**Price and installment transparency**
- [S] OJK's consumer-education site explains that interest schemes differ (for example fixed for an initial period, then floating), so borrowers should check which applies over the whole term. — [Sikapi Uangmu OJK](https://sikapiuangmu.ojk.go.id/FrontEnd/CMS/Article/20649)
- [S] ANTARA warns that real installments can differ from simulations because of rate changes, admin fees, insurance, provision, notary fees and bank terms. — [ANTARA](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4390630/cara-menghitung-cicilan-kredit-rumah-berdasarkan-suku-bunga)
- [S] Buyer costs beyond the down payment include BPHTB, name-transfer, notary and AJB fees; one ANTARA piece estimated roughly 8-10% of property value. The search summary did not identify which ANTARA article gave the estimate; treat the percentage as unverified. — [ANTARA (candidate article)](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5855027/gaji-rp5-juta-bisa-beli-rumah-cek-simulasi-kpr-berikut)
- [S][WEAK] Banks generally do not finance bare land with a normal KPR; they use a separate land loan (KPT / kredit kavling) and typically require an already split SHM/SHGB certificate free of disputes and encumbrance. — [Panduan Keuangan](https://panduankeuangan.id/kpr/kpr-tanah-kavling-kosong-syarat)

### Inferences
Prioritized buyer needs (my synthesis; the ranking is not measured by any source):
- **P0, legal status per plot:** show right type (SHM/SHGB, plus SHGB expiry), whether the certificate is split per plot or still a parent certificate, the holder name, permit/site-plan status, and what document the buyer gets at each stage (booking, PPJB, AJB, certificate), with expected timing. Explain each term in plain Indonesian where it appears. Offer an "verify it yourself" path (Sentuh Tanahku sharing, PPAT/notary). Avoid a bare "legal and safe" badge with nothing checkable behind it. Never show a status the record does not support.
- **P0, total price before contact:** cash price, down payment, booking fee and its refund terms, tenor, monthly amount, whether it changes, late penalty, cancellation/refund rules, and items excluded (taxes, notary, AJB, name transfer, certificate split). Label simulations as estimates with their assumptions. For developer in-house installments do not borrow KPR floating-rate wording unless it applies.
- **P0, current availability and plot facts:** plot number, dimensions, area, road access, position, price, status and the date last updated.
- **P1, developer identity and outside proof:** legal entity name, physical office, official contact numbers, official payment account in the company's name, dated site photos and progress, links to outside presence.
- **P1, location and risk:** pin and directions, distances, flood and terrain information.
- The Art. 146 question should go to a lawyer or notary; it affects how bare-plot sales are described. This note is not legal advice and does not establish whether it applies to any particular seller.

## What do usability studies say about property listing/detail pages, maps, filtering, comparison, and contact forms (including forced login)?

### Takeaway
Let people browse without an account, keep map and list synchronized (split view on large screens; list default with a map toggle on older mobile guidance), support both filtering and side-by-side comparison, and ask for as little as possible with a reason for each sensitive field. For post-purchase, offer both a pull status tracker and push updates in plain language. I found no independent usability study of interactive plot site plans or of favorites in property apps.

### Cited Findings

**Maps and list (mostly non-property or older)**
- [F] Baymard (Collins, 8 Feb 2022; hotel and rental search, apparently desktop): 70% of OTA and large hotel-brand sites defaulted to list view without a map; 65% of users on those sites did not use a map when comparing, being unable to find it or overlooking it. Switching views was hard; at Expedia, 75% of users who zoomed the map and returned to the list got a list that ignored the zoomed area. — [Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/accommodations-split-view)
- [F] Same article: split view (list plus interactive map) is recommended; 95% of test users engaged with Hilton's split-view map to narrow results. Pitfalls: overlay-style split views mistaken for the full results page; vertical filter sidebars crowding listings; ads, which also let the map cover key details such as price. It does not cover mobile layouts. — [Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/accommodations-split-view)
- [F] NN/g (Harley, **2014**, store locators; dated): list by default with a map toggle in apps; show distances; avoid swipe conflict between map panning and page scrolling (side gutters or hide/show map); size and space pins for touch; numbered cluster pins confused users; slow map rendering made users repeat gestures and overshoot. — [NN/g, mobile maps](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-maps-locations/)
- [S] A Baymard event-ticketing study found participants struggled when venue maps were static images or did not highlight the selected listing's exact location. The summary did not pin the finding to a URL beyond this article. — [Baymard](https://baymard.com/research-articles/event-ticketing-ux-research-launch)

**Interactive site plans and availability**
- [S][WEAK, vendor] Lot-map vendors present live availability as the core value ("which lots are open, which are under contract") and say static PDF plans go stale. They describe color-coded statuses (one product lists eight, including Available, Reserved, Under Contract, Sold), per-lot price and plan, pinch-zoom and tap-to-inquire on phones. No independent test data. — [ECI LotVue](https://www.ecisolutions.com/products/lotvue/features/interactive-site-maps/), [PlotAtlas](https://plotatlas.io/), [R2U](https://r2u.io/en/blog/interactive-site-plan-software-home-builders/)

**Filtering and comparison**
- [S] NN/g: filters/facets support noncompensatory decisions (eliminating options); comparison tables support compensatory decisions (trading off attributes), work for up to about 5 items, and most often fail on content (missing or inconsistent data). — [NN/g, comparison tables](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/comparison-tables/)
- [S] NN/g: filters should be discoverable, quick, powerful and transparent in what they apply. — [NN/g, data tables](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/data-tables/)

**Forced login**
- [F] NN/g (**2014**): login walls before content carry high interaction cost and defy reciprocity; users cannot judge value first; sites visited rarely are a poor fit. Exceptions include highly personal apps such as banking or email. At checkout, offer guest checkout and suggest an account afterwards. — [NN/g, Login Walls](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/login-walls/)
- [S] NN/g mobile registration guidance: think twice before requiring login; reduce typing (social sign-in, minimal fields, show password, fingerprint); prefer a texted code over an emailed link to avoid app switching. — [NN/g checklist](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/checklist-registration-login/)
- [S] NN/g's forms framework recommends guest completion with password creation deferred to confirmation. — [NN/g EAS](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/eas-framework-simplify-forms/)

**Contact forms (e-commerce checkout evidence, not property leads)**
- [F] Baymard (Scott, 2020, updated 29 Jul 2025): shoppers hesitate to give phone numbers (privacy, marketing calls); some testers faced with an unexplained required phone field entered fake numbers or abandoned. A short visible reason eases most concerns; otherwise make it optional or remove it. 39% of benchmarked sites require phone without explanation; in a survey of 1,026 shoppers, 14% would never give an online store their number. — [Baymard](https://baymard.com/blog/explain-phone-number-field)
- [S] Baymard: mark both required and optional fields explicitly, and do not split one input such as a phone number into several fields. — [required/optional](https://baymard.com/blog/required-optional-form-fields), [single inputs](https://baymard.com/blog/mobile-form-usability-single-input-fields)

**Post-purchase progress tracking (general service evidence)**
- [F] NN/g (3 Feb 2019), status trackers: reachable from the customer account; latest update first; plain language with no internal codes; scannable; previous updates visible; regular updates in long processes even if minor; reliable and accurate; consistent across channels including the contact center; announced at purchase; clear about any reference number needed; specific login errors; user control of contact details and frequency. — [NN/g, status trackers](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/status-tracker-progress-update/)
- [F] Same article, push updates: link straight to the detail; let users choose types; avoid duplicates and excess; allow opt-out from the message. — [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/status-tracker-progress-update/)
- [S] NN/g notes that offering only a pull tracker, without push updates, may leave users anxious. — [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/status-tracker-progress-update/)

### Inferences
Prioritized recommendations (transfers from adjacent domains; test with real buyers):
- **P0:** Browse the site plan, plot detail, price and legal summary without login. Ask for identity only when it protects something personal (booking, payments, own documents). If favoriting is offered to guests, keep it locally and merge after sign-in; this follows from the login-wall and guest-path guidance, and no source tested favorites.
- **P0:** Status on the map must use color plus a text label or pattern and a legend, match the list and detail page, and show when it was last updated. A plot that shows available but is taken is likely to damage trust (follows from "current and correct content").
- **P0:** On phones, give the site plan a searchable/filterable list equivalent, preserve filter, zoom and selection when switching or going back, make small plots tappable (zoom plus a bottom sheet), avoid gesture conflict with page scroll, and highlight the selected plot. The 2014 list-default advice concerns location finders; for a site plan, where adjacency and position are the product, a map-first view may be right if the list is one tap away. Needs testing.
- **P1:** Filters for status, price or monthly amount, area, block and corner position; compare 2-4 plots with identical attribute rows including legal status and total cost.
- **P1:** Inquiry should need no form, or at most name plus one contact with a stated reason and what happens next.
- **P1:** After purchase, one timeline covering both money (paid, due, receipts) and deliverables (PPJB, split certificate, AJB, handover, site progress), with dates, the latest item first, evidence (dated photos, documents), honest delay notices and a named contact.

## What is specific to Indonesian users (WhatsApp as channel, low-to-mid Android devices, network quality, land legality terms such as SHM/SHGB, fraud concerns)?

### Takeaway
Indonesian access is mainly by smartphone and largely over mobile data, WhatsApp is very widely used, and scam losses reported to OJK are large, so a fast light mobile experience, an easy WhatsApp handoff and verifiable official identity matter. Hard Indonesia-specific data on WhatsApp property inquiries and on device RAM was not found. Legal terms, rules and verification tools are in the first section.

### Cited Findings

**Connectivity and devices**
- [S] APJII 2025 survey (fieldwork 10 April to 16 July 2025, 8,700 respondents aged 13+, 38 provinces): penetration 80.66%, about 229.4 million users. — [Bisnis](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20250806/101/1899997/data-apjii-pengguna-internet-di-indonesia-tembus-22943-juta-orang-pada-2025)
- [S] Reports of the same survey put smartphone access at about 83% (83.39% in one outlet, 83.29% in another) and mobile data as the main connection at 68% versus Wi-Fi 28%. Confirm in the APJII report, including the denominator. — [Infodigital](https://infodigital.co.id/83-orang-ri-akses-internet-gunakan-ponsel/), [VOI](https://voi.id/teknologi/502668/survei-apjii-2025-80-66-persen-warga-indonesia-sudah-terkoneksi-internet)
- [S] Statcounter, Indonesia, mobile Android versions, Oct 2025 to Oct 2026: Android 16 about 27.8%, 15 about 17.7%, 14 about 13.5%, 13 about 11.4%, leaving roughly 30% on other (mostly older) versions (that remainder is my subtraction and may include unknown versions). **Fraud climate**

- [S] As reported 5 November 2026: IASC had blocked Rp967.5 billion in funds through 28 October 2026. — [ANTARA](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5834751/ojk-iasc-blokir-rp9675-miliar-dana-terkait-penipuan)
- [S] Complaint channels include BPKN (pengaduan.bpkn.go.id), YLKI (pelayanan.ylki.or.id) and a housing ministry WhatsApp line reported as 0812-88888-911; verify the number officially before displaying it. — [Koran Properti](https://koranproperti.com/dirugikan-developer-lapor-ke-nomor-benar-pkp-0812-88888-911-pemerintah-tegas-berantas-mafia-pengembang/)

**Legality terms and verification:** see SHM/SHGB, PPJB/AJB, PP 12/2021, Art. 146 and Sentuh Tanahku in the first section (sources: [ANTARA](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/4786221/pahami-perbedaan-shm-dan-shgb-sebelum-membeli-tanah-atau-rumah), [ATR/BPN](https://www.atrbpn.go.id/berita/cek-informasi-tanah-sebelum-membeli-manfaatkan-fitur-berbagi-akses-di-aplikasi-sentuh-tanahku)).

### Inferences
- **P0, performance:** the site plan is likely the heaviest screen. Show a lightweight first view (list or low-resolution plan) quickly, load detail, panoramas and photos on demand, compress and cache images, show skeletons and retry states, and label cached availability with its time. Test on a low-to-mid Android phone over throttled mobile data. This is inferred from smartphone and mobile-data reliance; no source measured device RAM or plot-app performance.
- **P0, WhatsApp handoff:** a persistent contact action on plot detail that pre-fills plot block/number and the question, so the buyer sees and can edit the message before sending. Do not require registration or a form first. Keep a phone fallback. Chat history should not be the only record of price, booking or status.
- **P0, anti-impersonation:** publish official WhatsApp numbers and the company-name payment account inside the app, warn against transfers to personal accounts, issue verifiable receipts and show recorded payments in the buyer's account.
