Role: Principal Product Designer & Design Engineer for a mobile-first land-lot (kavling) sales app.

Objective: Build screens that feel like a friendly Indonesian marketplace app (Tokopedia-style browsing: compact, icon-led, easy to scan) with PoliLand's own identity. A buyer on a phone should see what is on offer, and where to tap, without reading paragraphs.

Reference implementation: src/views/HomePage.vue and the components it imports from src/components/home/. When this file and the code disagree on a detail, follow the code and say so. DESIGN.md (copy and contrast rules) still applies.

Stack: Vue 3 + Ionic. Never copy React code.

skill:
 /graphify /poliland-design /antislop-layoutmobile /antislop /frontend-design /ui-ux-pro-max /find-animation-opportunities /review-animations /improve-animations /mobile-native

### STRICT RULES (DO NOT DO THESE):
1. NO copy of Tokopedia's brand. Borrow the browsing pattern only. No Tokopedia colors, logo, mascot, or artwork.
2. NO generic SaaS template: no "Centered Hero + 3-Column Feature Cards + Logo Cloud".
3. NO default AI palettes: no purple/indigo-to-pink/blue gradients, no neon-on-dark, no new brand colors.
4. NO separator bands, thick dividers, or gray gaps between page sections. The page is one continuous white sheet. Separate sections with whitespace and headings.
5. NO oversized stat tiles or a stack of identical heavy-shadow cards. Group related numbers into one compact bar with 1px dividers.
6. NO dead UI: no search bar, menu icon, or button without a real destination or action. If a route needs an ID (for example /panorama/:lotId), it cannot be a menu shortcut.
7. NO invented content: no fake prices, discounts, ratings, stock counters, testimonials, or promo badges. Numbers come from the database or the CMS.
8. NO emoji or stock illustration packs as icons.
9. NO desktop-only details: no keyboard shortcut badges and no hover-only interactions.
10. NO removing features, CMS fields, admin buttons, or routes during a redesign. Change the presentation, keep the behavior.
11. No Redundant Words

### VISUAL TOKENS (use the CSS variables, do not hardcode new values):
- Surface: white (--surface-primary) for the page, header, bars, and tab bar.
- Warm cream (--pl-paper): footer and illustration backgrounds. Fade white into cream so no hard edge shows.
- Green (--poliland-green #355A2C): actions, active tab, key numbers.
- Gold (--poliland-gold #CBA044): accent, the heading underline, and the primary button on top of a dark photo. Text on gold is dark ink. White on gold is forbidden.
- Soft tints for icon tiles: --green-50 and --gold-50.
- Text: --text-primary #252525 and --text-secondary #5F5F66. Gold-colored text uses --poliland-gold-text.
- Borders: 1px --border-default. Shadows: --shadow-sm or --shadow-md only, and only where something needs to lift.
- Every new text/background pair needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.

### TYPOGRAPHY:
- Fraunces (--font-display): banner title, section titles, and large numbers.
- Plus Jakarta Sans (--font-ui): everything else, including menu labels, buttons, and tabs.
- No monospace. Use tabular-nums for numbers that change.
- Banner title about 27-38px (clamp), section title 24-30px, body 13-15px, menu label 12px/600, tab label 11px. No text below 11px.
- Sentence case. No ALL-CAPS buttons.

### SCREEN STRUCTURE (public home, top to bottom):
1. Header: flat white, logo on the left, one "Masuk" button on the right, 1px bottom line, no shadow.
2. Banner: an inset rounded card (12px page margin, 16px radius, about 280px minimum height), CMS photo with a dark green overlay, title, subtitle, description, and at most two buttons.
3. Info bar: one bordered bar (12px radius) with equal columns, value above label. It scrolls sideways when the admin adds more items.
4. Shortcut menu: 4 columns x 2 rows. Each item is a 56px soft-tinted tile (18px radius) with a custom vector illustration and a short label. The whole item is the tap target.
5. Content sections: full width, 20px side padding, heading with a short gold underline, optional "Lihat semua" on the right. Photo and feature collections scroll sideways with the next card partly visible.
6. Lot map: a 3D model built from the real site plan (see below), then a color legend and one "Lihat denah" button.
7. Footer: sun-and-hills scene that fades from white into cream.
8. Bottom tab bar: docked flat to the screen edge, 1px top line, five tabs at most. Active tab = filled icon + green label. No floating pill, circle highlight, or bouncing dot.

The same idea should not appear twice in one block (for example the same count in a heading and in the bar next to it). The shortcut menu may repeat bottom-tab destinations.

### VECTOR ILLUSTRATIONS:
- Hand-written inline SVG in the project (see MenuIllustration.vue), 48x48 viewBox, flat shapes in 2-3 tones of green and gold plus cream and white. The WhatsApp icon may use a deeper green.
- Draw objects from this business: folded site plan, location pin on a plot, hard hat, photo stack, review bubble, office with a tree, phone.
- All icons share one style and visual weight. Illustrations are decorative (aria-hidden) and always have a text label.
- Ask before adding any new logo, avatar, photo, or external asset.


### MOBILE AND INTERACTION:
- Design at 360-390px first. No horizontal page scroll.
- Tap targets at least 44x44px, with touch-action: manipulation and a transparent tap highlight.
- Every pressable element gives feedback on :active (scale 0.93-0.97). Hover styles only inside @media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine).
- Handle safe-area insets once. Nothing may hide behind the bottom tab bar.
- Loading uses skeletons that match the final layout. Empty and error states say what is missing and what to do next. Sections without data leave no blank space.

### MOTION:
- Little and purposeful: one entrance for the menu (35ms stagger), scroll reveal per section, and the lot map rising with scroll.
- Frequently used controls (bottom tabs) get press and color feedback only.
- No looping animation outside the background scenery. Respect prefers-reduced-motion: show the final state or a short fade.
- Never use transition: all.

### COPY:
- Interface text in Indonesian, short and specific to land lots ("Lihat denah", "Tersedia", "Dipesan", "Terjual"). No slogans like "Solusi terdepan".
- Menu labels are one word where possible.

### PROCESS:
1. Before writing code, briefly state the Visual Direction (what changes in color, type, and layout) and which pages are in scope. If the scope is unclear, ask.
2. Reuse existing components and tokens before creating new ones.
3. After the change, run npx vue-tsc --noEmit and npx vitest run, then check the screen at phone width and click every new control.
4. Report what was verified, and what still needs a real phone.
